b. Non-Personal Information

c. Sensitive Personal Information

Sensitive information will only be collected with your explicit consent and for specific purposes such as legal compliance or enhanced security.

  • To respond to your inquiries, feedback, and support requests.
  • To personalize your experience and provide targeted services.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and fraud.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax and regulatory reporting.
  • To conduct research, analytics, and internal audits to improve the Services.

3. Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, we process your personal data based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes stated in this Privacy Policy or as required by law. Once data is no longer needed, it will be securely deleted or anonymized.

UsiScan implements reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

7. Your Rights under Philippine Law

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the following rights:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know how your data is collected, used, and processed.
  • Right to Access: You may request access to your personal data held by UsiScan.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure/Blocking: You may request deletion or restriction of processing your data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You may object to the processing of your data for marketing or other purposes allowed by law.
  • Right to File a Complaint: You may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) regarding any breach of your data privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, you may contact us at privacy@usiscan.ph.
